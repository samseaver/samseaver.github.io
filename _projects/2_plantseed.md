**PlantSEED** enables automated functional annotation and reconstruction of plant primary
metabolism, with consistent subcellular compartmentalization across species. It is integrated into
DOE's KBase and is now being refactored into a new v3 codebase.

- **Role:** first author of the PlantSEED resource papers (*PNAS*, 2014; *The Plant Journal*, 2018).
- **Impact:** turns a plant genome into a curated, comparable genome-scale metabolic model.
- **Links:** [GitHub](https://github.com/ModelSEED/PlantSEED) · see the publications page.
