The **DOE Systems Biology Knowledgebase (KBase)** is an open platform for predictive biology. As a
core member of its **plants team**, I build the plant metabolic-modeling **apps** and **integrate the
plant data** that let researchers reconstruct, simulate, and analyze metabolic models alongside their
'omics data.

- **Role:** co-author of the KBase platform paper (*Nature Biotechnology*, 2018; >1,900 citations)
  and senior/key personnel on the DOE KBase program.
- **Links:** [kbase.us](https://www.kbase.us) · see the publications page.
